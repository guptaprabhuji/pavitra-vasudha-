Zero-Cost Organic Humus

DIY Home Composting in 4 Easy Steps

Turn 100% of your daily kitchen wet waste into nutrient-dense black gold for your balcony plants.

30-Day Cycle
1

Chop & Collect

Chop fruit and vegetable scraps into small 2-3 cm pieces so microbes can digest them rapidly.

2

The 2:1 Layering Rule

For every 1 bucket of wet kitchen scraps (Green/Nitrogen), add 2 buckets of dry leaves or shredded egg trays (Brown/Carbon).

3

Aeration & Moisture

Turn the pile once a week with a trowel for oxygen flow. Keep it as damp as a wrung-out sponge.

4

Harvest Sweet Humus

In 30-40 days, the volume shrinks by 70%, turning into rich, dark, sweet-smelling organic soil amendment.
